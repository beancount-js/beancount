# Review

Review surfaces present host-produced proposals before they change the ledger.
The controller retains selection, revision checks, apply/cancel actions, and
route history; sheets receive typed records and callbacks.

The routed experience is composed by `BeancountApplication`. Public ingestion
exports such as `IngestionTable` and `IngestionMerchantReview` can also be
embedded by a host that owns its own route and shell.

## Catalog coverage

The stories cover transaction review, proposal details, account assignment,
merchant review, reconciliation, grouped dates, narrow layouts, and the
selected-row count treatment.
